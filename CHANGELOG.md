# Changelog

## 0.2.0 — 2026-09-19

- Memory now defaults to second-person narration and offers a close-friend voice on request, using casual language, short sentences, and conversational spacing.
- Memory explicitly welcomes metaphor, humor, and the AI narrator's impressions while preserving the experiences shared. Privacy and sharing rules remain separate.
- Making and Legacy explicitly describe the AI's role as narrator. Both keep third-person defaults without proactively offering a perspective change.
- Legacy now introduces personal life histories alongside stories about loved ones; one period of life is enough to begin.
- Legacy and Fiction have shorter process instructions with more room to adapt to the conversation. Fiction retains its creative freedom and safety boundaries.
- All four skills clarify that unrelated requests should be handled normally, without forcing them into a writing task.

## 0.1.0 — 2026-09-18

Initial English release of Dialogue Memories, with four skills: Memory, Making, Legacy, and Fiction. Memory is the everyday entry point. Making and Legacy support documentary-like third-person accounts; Fiction supports both collaborative development and delegated writing.
